# Cover image brief — "The table that holds"

## The idea

One restaurant table is set and perfectly still, while the evening moves around it. Staff passing through the room are long-exposure blurs; the table, its candle and its place settings are sharp. The reservation is the still point. That is the product in one frame: the booking holds while everything around it changes.

AI is how Tablekeeper was made, not what it is. Nothing in the image should say "AI".

## Composition

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9. Deliver 1920 × 1080 at minimum (2400 × 1350 is better) as PNG or JPG. lablab's general guidance asks for 16:9 PNG or JPG; I could not open this event's form to confirm, so check it when uploading.
- **Camera:** a high three-quarter angle, about 50° down, at eye level for someone standing beside the table. It is not a flat top-down shot; you should see a little depth.
- **Subject placement:** the table sits on the **left third**, slightly below centre. It is set for three: three plates, simple stemware, folded linen napkins, one short candle in a brass holder, and a small folded reservation card with no readable text.
- **Motion:** two or three soft, faceless streaks of staff crossing behind and beside the table: dark aprons, a white plate held high. These are long-exposure blurs, not people.
- **Negative space:** the **right 40%** is a calm, darker wall of warm plaster or walnut panelling with gentle out-of-focus light. That is where the title goes.
- **Safe area:** keep the table and the title inside the central 80% of the frame, in case a card view crops the edges.

## Light and colour

- Warm tungsten evening light, around 2700 K. A low pendant lights the table; the room falls off into soft shadow, without crushed blacks.
- Palette: cream linen, terracotta (the app's accent), brass, walnut and a deep olive. No blue or purple cast.
- The feel of an editorial food-magazine photograph: natural grain, shallow depth of field, restrained.

## Title (added afterwards, never generated)

- **TABLEKEEPER** in a serif, in caps, with generous letter spacing, cream on the dark wall. Its cap height is about 9% of the frame height, so it reads at thumbnail size.
- Below it, smaller: *Reservations that survive reality.*
- Nothing else: no logos, no "powered by", no badges.

## Thumbnail test (do this before accepting the image)

Shrink the image to 320 × 180:

- You can still tell it is a warmly lit table waiting for guests.
- "TABLEKEEPER" is legible.
- The blur reads as movement, not dirt or a camera fault.

If any of these fails, simplify: less blur, a darker wall, a bigger title.

## Prompt to give the image model

> Editorial photograph, 16:9. A single restaurant table for three set for dinner in a quiet, upscale neighbourhood restaurant in the early evening: cream linen, three plates, simple stemware, folded napkins, one lit candle in a short brass holder, a small folded reservation card with no visible text. High three-quarter angle, about 50 degrees down. The table sits in the left third of the frame. Warm tungsten light (2700 K) from a low pendant above the table; the rest of the room falls into soft shadow. Around and behind the table, long-exposure motion blur of staff moving through the room — soft, faceless streaks of dark aprons and a white plate — so the table is perfectly still and sharp while the evening moves around it. Palette: cream, terracotta, brass, walnut, deep olive. The right 40% of the frame is a calm, darker, softly textured wall with gentle bokeh, left empty for a title. Natural film grain, shallow depth of field. No faces, no text, no logos, no screens, no phones.

## Do not use

- Robots, AI brains, circuit-board faces, glowing nodes or network graphs.
- Purple or neon gradients, cyberpunk lighting, glass cards floating in space, holograms.
- Terminal screens, code, "matrix" rain, chat bubbles, agent mascots.
- Phones or laptops showing a UI mock-up, fintech cards, coins, blockchain shapes.
- Smiling stock diners, faces of any kind, hands holding phones.
- Generated text, including on the reservation card. Image models garble it.
